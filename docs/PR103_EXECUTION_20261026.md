# PR103 amplitude-control execution contract

PR103 adds one amplitude-matched uniform-P wind input, `AMPLITUDE`, while preserving PR102 `STRUCTURED` as the comparison candidate. The frozen contrast uses stored REAL32 fields: `A = R32(H + s(Pstored − H))`, where `s = ||Nstored − Hstored||₂ / ||Pstored − Hstored||₂` on the predeclared free U/V faces. The proposal receipt distinguishes the ideal REAL64 value, the one REAL32 cast, the readback norm envelope, and the continuous PR99 reference proof. It does not change the physical approval or observation-skill state.

## Runner routes

`tools/pr86_time_response.py` exposes three separate PR103 arms:

- `AMPLITUDE` is one 640-second run using the exact base C2 executable, the frozen COUPLED template and boundary, and a candidate-bound `pr103_execution_preflight_v1`.
- `AMPLITUDE_BASELINE20` is the one-step same-input base-C2 control.
- `AMPLITUDE_CAPTURE` is the one-step diagnostic-overlay capture. It uses the existing ACA04 diagnostic executable and the same archived-consumer receipt bound to A.

All three arms require the source-bound PR103 proposal receipt. The capture and baseline are staged independently; their inputs, boundary, namelist, launch controls, and first-step outputs are compared by the receipt-bound noninterference assessment. The candidate source capture reuses the diagnostic executable already used for PR102 H/P/N. It must capture RU, RV, and MUDF and pass its same-input base-C2 noninterference comparison.

## Forecast preflight

The `pr103_execution_preflight_v1` record is a no-launch record. Its `READY_FOR_ROOT_REVIEW_NO_LAUNCH` state is not launch authorization. It binds the proposal and base-C2 build; all five closed controls OFF/HYDRO/COUPLED/PROJECTED/STRUCTURED; the new archived-consumer assessment with both `reference_constraint` and `source_arithmetic` passing for exact A; the A20 baseline/diagnostic comparison; and one-step H/P/N/A native source captures on the common diagnostic executable. The H/P/N capture evidence is reused from PR102, and the matched forecast controls are not rerun.

The runner rechecks each receipt, preflight, candidate, executable, and referenced evidence hash before launch and during finalization. It checks the forecast controls against the pinned base C2 executable, C2 boundary, 640-second namelist, and single-thread launch request. The source-capture norm summaries are descriptive and do not form a response-dependent admission threshold.

## Readback and limits

After the one new forecast closes, `tools/pr103_time_response.py` consumes the fixed-support history readback together with the five pinned controls and the closed A receipt. The native first-call comparison uses the existing PR98/PR99 readers and fixed masks, with A compared to H/P/N. PR102 `STRUCTURED` and its closed forecast receipt remain unchanged.

These outputs describe model response on the declared support. They do not establish observational skill, physical approval, continuity closure, or forecast improvement. The raw PR102 trace remains external; compact extraction of selected POST_KDM6 records still requires streaming the compressed trace in full.
