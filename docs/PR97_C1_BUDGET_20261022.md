The two selected C4 columns each contain 22 full-cloud evaporation events
and two partial-evaporation events in the general condensation/evaporation
branch. The retained raw label is `COND`, but its signed extent is negative
in all four general-branch events. Vapor increases and cloud water
decreases without clearing. This corrects the physical description only;

## Scope retained

The compact C4 longruns failed after 620 seconds, after a separately
verified 600-second prefix. Those failures are not C1 failures and are
classified from their own source/full diagnostics. C1's completed
600-second window does not test that later boundary.

Same-call KDM6 accounting uses its actual input DEN/DELZ and map area.
It is separate from the native hybrid host water budget and from energy
and mass–wind closure. Numerical store errors remain numerical terms,
not phase changes or water corrections. No independent observation skill
is approved by completion or by smaller OFF–COUPLED differences.
