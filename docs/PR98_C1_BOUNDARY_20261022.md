## Audit rules and open result

The closed receipts bind the executable, source inputs, schedule and wall
times listed above. The auditors keep generic Q/N/B, exact source endpoint,
active-ice policy and procedural completion separate. They require the final
640-second checkpoint; all three runs stopped at step 31, so that gate remains
incomplete. The first 600 seconds are compared with the matching C1 receipts
using the 12-field trace comparator and ten raw history fields. These frozen
comparators use `numpy.array_equal` for numerical exactness; they do not claim
whole-NetCDF or record-byte identity, and history comparison ends at the last
timestamp present in both receipts.

The first grouped transaction subpredicate remains **OPEN**. The captured
logs identify the PR87 full joint moment candidate as infeasible but do not
identify the initial grouped rejection predicate. Per-cell callback records
are separate. A new failure-only diagnostic fixture would be required to
identify that predicate; no such instrumentation was included in this frozen
run. This boundary test does not address the separate C4 620-second failure
or infer a cause from a generic audit finding.

Scientific approval remains open regardless of procedural completion.
