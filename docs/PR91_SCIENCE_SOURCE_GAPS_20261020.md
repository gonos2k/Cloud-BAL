## C7 AUT/G deferral attribution

The PR90 area-weighted collector is valid for aggregate request activity: over completed steps 1–9, AUT deferred 84.658% (OFF) / 84.658% (COUPLED) of proposed half-L1 and G deferred 86.438% / 86.417%. It is weighted by local `DEN*DELZ` and grid cell area. These are group-level requested-vector diagnostics, not process throughput or causal categories.

The compiled PR90 KDM6 source and same-run `pr87_admission_reason.raw.gz` do carry a useful but narrower category: for each AUT/G partial admission, the ledger records the **first failed full-proposal endpoint check** (plus alpha and a mass-activity proxy). A read-only tally finds AUT endpoint reason 6 (RAIN) in all 46,831 OFF and 46,838 COUPLED rows. For G, the largest category is reason 9 (BULKDENSITY): 119,963/122,846 OFF rows and 119,971/122,852 COUPLED rows; smaller counts are RAIN 1,358/1,355, DONORNEGATIVE 523/523, ORPHAN 759/760, ICE 242/242, and PSDCLOUD 1/1. All recorded vapor reasons are 0. The logger is gated by `pr67_trace_this_call = .not. pr67_trace_complete`, and KDM6 sets the saved completion flag on exit. Its record schema has no model timestep or trace-context identifier, so temporal coverage cannot be joined to the nine-step collector. Treat this as trace-gated evidence, not an all-step tally.

The logged `mass_activity_kg_per_kgdry` field is a mixing-ratio half-L1 proxy, **without** `DEN*DELZ` or horizontal area. At the AUT write, source sets it to `0.5*sum(abs(rain_aut_q_process))` (five hydrometeor Q components; AUT QV is zero). At the G write it is `0.5*(sum(abs(rain_g_q_process))+abs(rain_g_qv_process))` (five Q components plus QV). It excludes number, rime-volume, and temperature components. The same record has `rhoDry_kg_m3`, `DELZ_m`, alpha, group, local `i`, `lat`/j, `k`, and `loop`; it also has status, endpoint/vapor reason and full-evaporation-pending flag, but no model timestep. Numerical fields use `ES24.16` formatting from REAL32 source values, so integrations from printed decimal records are approximate at the stored-real/decimal-print precision.

For a bounded **trace-gated proxy**, I joined records to their same-run history `MAPFAC_MX/MY` using `A=DX*DY/(MAPFAC_MX*MAPFAC_MY)`. Map factors are exactly static over each nine-record history window. Each row contributes `m*rhoDry*DELZ*A` to full-request-associated activity, `alpha` times that to alpha-scaled activity, and `(1-alpha)` times that to withheld activity.
