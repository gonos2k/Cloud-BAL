# PR82 native NC number-basis trace

The clean PR80 replay confirms that QNC is created during the completed scalar-update state transition before the first KDM6 call. It does not identify which term in that scalar update creates the value; its tendency and geometry operands remain unobserved.

At global `(i,j,k)=(4,2,22)` (Fortran field index `(i,k,j)=(4,22,2)`), `wrfinput_d01` and the initial `wrfout` readback both contain QNCLOUD=0 and QCLOUD=0. The scalar observer then records QNC=0 at PRE_RK, after RK preparation, before and after physics/PBL, and before/after the RK1 tendency and update. After the RK2 tendency call, QNC is still zero. The first changed state is immediately after the RK2 `rk_update_scalar` call: QNC becomes negative subnormal `0x80000010`. The RK3 update changes it to the smallest positive REAL32 subnormal, `0x00000001`; QCLOUD remains zero at every recorded boundary.

The first KDM6 conversion reports:

```text
NUMBER_BASIS_REJECT,NC,INPUT,4,2,22,00000001,3EE46C18,00000000,00001932,00001932,...,POSITIVE_TO_ZERO
```

The input NC bit pattern is the minimum positive REAL32 subnormal. At dry density `0.4461371898651123` (`0x3EE46C18`), its exact product from the represented REAL32 operands is `6.251713590361712e-46`, below the round-to-zero midpoint `7.006492321624085e-46`. The product is zero with MXCSR `0x1932` before and after the conversion, with FTZ and DAZ clear. This is an unavoidable REAL32 representability failure for that input and density; the observer makes no value changes.

The source contract is in [the read-only `rk_update_scalar` excerpt](evidence/pr82_rk_update_scalar_source_excerpt_20261012.md). For NC, `rk_update_scalar` combines `advect_tend*msfty` with `sc_tend`, then applies the RK scalar quotient using `scalar_1`, `c1/c2`, old/new/base dry mass, and `dt_rk`. Therefore the observed post-call mutation localizes the first changed field state to this completed operation, while leaving open the source and signs/magnitudes of both tendency terms and the geometry factors. In particular, unchanged QNC snapshots across physics/PBL or `rk_scalar_tend` do not rule out those stages contributing through `sc_tend` or `advect_tend`. No physical correction should target a term until those exact operands at this cell are captured.
