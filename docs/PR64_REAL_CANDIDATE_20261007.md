# PR64 actual NE57 phase candidate (2026-10-07)

## Scope

This is a conditional thermodynamic feasibility trial on the real NE57 background for 2026-08-16 12 UTC. The prior is the immutable canonical background read by `read_real_shadow_state`; the trial uses the existing pressure-analysis and column-physics pipeline, physical contract evaluator, SHADOW writer, and independent readers. Two scopes are recorded separately: the phase-only contract run and the follow-on phase-plus-geopotential (Phi) run.

Before either run, the fixture fixed cell `(i,j,k)=(143,191,9)` and the existing liquid saturation policy at `RH=1`. The cell is above ground and has valid pressure, temperature, vapor, and cloud water. The single-cell phase extent is independently bisected from those background values. No external source or boundary increment is declared. Physical source and boundary authority is not established by this conditional trial.

To avoid the unrelated radar retrieval path changing precipitation, omega targets, or provenance, the analysis input explicitly withholds radar reflectivity and radar LOS observations and categorical cloud observations. Radar reflectivity uses the canonical missing encoding (`valid=false`, `quality=QUALITY_RAW_MISSING`, `source=0`, `value=0`). The original background object and all physical background arrays remain the prior. The raw input records are not edited. Thus this is not the original all-observation producer candidate.

The checked grid has `235 × 283 × 22` cells and full atmospheric contract coverage of 1,294,186 cells. The prescribed internal phase exchange changes only vapor and cloud water at the predeclared cell; source and boundary terms remain zero. The phase-only O0/O2 artifacts are byte-identical and passed the independent physical-contract reader.

The separate phase-plus-Phi candidate uses the same thermodynamic trial, fixed winds, and fixed pressure geometry. Its Phi support is the selected column's represented levels `k=4..22`; the datum is the lowest supported background center `k=4`, chosen from the actual above-ground support. The existing hydrostatic residual evaluator assesses all 18 adjacent common layers before and after the pipeline. A storage bound of four times the largest float32 spacing in background Phi over the support was declared before execution (`0.0625 m² s⁻²`). Both profile candidate processes report 18 common layers, background/candidate RMS residuals `45.311419733489466` / `45.311435602178300 m² s⁻²`, maximum per-layer residual change `0.00390625 m² s⁻²`, and 14 changed Phi cells. The maximum residual change is within the predeclared storage bound. However, both phase-plus-Phi receipts are `FAIL_OPEN`: the independent physical-contract reader passed, but the independent Phi mutation reader raised a test-harness `ValueError` in its malformed-dimension case. Those original full-run receipts alone do not establish a passed Phi serialization/readback gate. The separate corrected normal replay and storage-tail coverage are recorded below; the candidate remains outside full physical approval.

Every receipt records the six raw input hashes, static-grid hash, pinned compiler profile and flags, NetCDF configuration/archive hashes, all compiled source hashes (including the fixture program), object and executable hashes, runner and independent-reader hashes, pre/post guards, and exit statuses. The phase-plus-Phi receipts also bind the shared validator and pressure-Phi checker hashes. The source and runner were uncommitted during these runs, but their bytes were hash-frozen before build and checked again after execution; this is not a clean-commit build. A later commit must contain those exact recorded bytes to bind the results to the commit.
