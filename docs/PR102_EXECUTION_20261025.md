# PR102 wind-structure execution contract

PR102 uses a separate `STRUCTURED` arm for the matched 640-second forecast. It stages the same pinned C2 executable, COUPLED template, boundary, namelist, and runner controls as the historical PR101 `PROJECTED` arm. The separate label preserves the historical PR99/PR101 proposal schema and receipts.

## Proposal receipt

`pr102_wind_structure_proposal_v1` remains a proposal with status `PROPOSAL_REQUIRES_NATIVE_CONSUMER_AND_SOURCE_CAPTURE`. It binds `candidate`, `original_p`, `reference_hydro`, `reference_coupled`, `boundary`, `generator`, `policy`, and `composition` as `{path, sha256}` artifacts. `original_p` is the byte-exact PR99 projected COUPLED input and candidate parent. `reference_hydro` plus `reference_coupled` form the source pair. `composition_sha256` must equal the composition artifact hash. The receipt declares only U/V changes and byte-identical non-target variables. The runner verifies all artifact hashes, proposal role/status, this contract, the COUPLED parent, and boundary.

Consumer and native source-capture receipts are deliberately separate from the proposal to avoid circular dependencies: they can only be generated after the candidate exists. The 640-second runner requires the PR102 preflight receipt, which binds both prerequisites to the exact candidate and C2 executable and includes their artifact hashes for rechecking before launch and final receipt.

## Pre-640 gates

The read-only preflight requires:

1. A completed archived `calc_ww_cp` consumer check bound to the candidate, with a passing dry-mass check.
2. Completed one-step native source captures for HYDRO, original P, and the new candidate, all on one identical C2 diagnostic executable. Each must bind its exact input hash, record a noninterference pass, capture `RU`, `RV`, and `MUDF`, and bind the same diagnostic overlay receipt.
3. The four existing closed OFF, HYDRO, COUPLED, and PROJECTED C2 640-second receipts, all at 32 steps and matching the pinned executable, boundary, and namelist. The proposal's H/C references and original P input must match those receipts exactly.

The PR98 source observer is the starting point for full RU/RV and dry-mass array capture. Its historical `pr98_prepare_native_capture.py` is pinned to C1 sources, so its output cannot be presented as a C2 capture. The PR102 C2 additive overlay plan derives source edits from exact C2 `module_em.F` and `solve_em.F` receipts, compiles only those two changed objects, and reuses the PR98 private writer routines embedded in `module_em.F`. It preserves the other seven C2 override objects, archive members, inherited modules, libraries, and link flags. PR101's selected two-face U/V diffusion capture is not sufficient for these gates.

The consumer and source-capture gates are diagnostic checks, not physical approval. In particular, captured MUDF is the native combined DMDT+MU_TEND output; it does not isolate every dry-mass flux term or establish continuity closure. The native momentum arrays remain source-coordinate diagnostics, not energy or acceleration closure.
