# PR101 upper U/V horizontal-diffusion response

## Result

The 20-second paired HYDRO and projected-P20 captures used the same
instrumented C2-derived executable, inputs bound by the existing HYDRO and
PR99 P20 receipts, the same boundary, and the same namelist. Both completed
one timestep to 13:00:20. The first captured U/V calls were at native
`time_step=20` seconds and `rk_step=1`.

For U `(136,16)` and V `(135,17)`, captured `field`, `XKMHD`, `MUT`, `c1/c2`,
map metrics, `khdif`, `rdx/rdy`, options, flags, and bounds are identical
between arms except for `field`. The target face field differences at k=37–39
are +7.84588 m/s for U and −7.22398 m/s for V. The source-form factor split
therefore assigns the complete P−H diffusion-increment difference to the field
stencil for these events; coefficient effects and interaction are zero.

| Face | P−H field, k=37–39 (m/s) | P−H HD tendency increment, k=37–39 |
|---|---:|---:|
| U `(136,16)` | +7.84588 at each level | −8666.12, −10319.85, −11229.59 |
| V `(135,17)` | −7.22398 at each level | +5411.82, +6444.83, +7013.13 |

These increments are native tendency units, not m/s or acceleration. At k=39,
the P/H increments are −11227.68/+1.91519 for U and +7012.77/−0.35565 for V.
The source-form Fortran replay reproduced all six P20 and all six HYDRO POST
REAL32 values bitwise. Evidence is in
[`docs/evidence/pr101_upper_wind_20261023/`](evidence/pr101_upper_wind_20261023/).

## Source and operator

The C2-derived overlay executable is SHA256
`4100279548fa19c5edd591f0738d3555aa8655b886bc2c9b9da7c88cd202615d` (the
base C2 executable is `387f46df03e87b8693a57fdefc4ddb62a848e7a946ce417ee8b3d54d2c62cc9a`).
The paired H and P20 runs use that same instrumented executable. The source
operator routine `horizontal_diffusion` is SHA256
`27ce690b27b24c80247a5551b48d37fc32fc47470d806cbef1bb859d58edc31c`.
`module_big_step_utilities_em.o` has identical SHA256
`8f7a0666664bdccb45c414cb9e6ec481f7daaad3fa6170f0d51f524515572343` in the
base and instrumented C2 archives. See
[`horizontal_diffusion_object_compare.json`](evidence/pr101_upper_wind_20261023/horizontal_diffusion_object_compare.json).

`solve_em` supplies `grid%u_2` and `grid%v_2` directly to the `INTENT(IN)`
`rk_tendency` arguments `u` and `v`; in `forward_step`, the U/V diffusion
calls use those arrays when `rk_step==1` and `diff_opt==1`. No remap occurs
between those arguments and the calls. The captured namelist options are
`diff_opt=1`, `km_opt=4`, and `diff_6th_opt=0`; all captured open/nesting/
periodic/polar flags and loop bounds match between arms.

The source adds a metric-weighted horizontal flux divergence to the tendency:

`r_f += m_x [K_x^+(f_{i+1}-f_i)-K_x^-(f_i-f_{i-1})] + m_y [K_y^+(f_{j+1}-f_j)-K_y^-(f_j-f_{j-1})]`.

The U and V branches use their native face metrics. The coefficient terms use
`MUT`, `c1/c2`, `XKMHD`, map metrics, and `rdx/rdy`. At k=39, `khdif=0`, but
the captured `XKMHD` is 125959.43 for U and 126001.77 for V. The pinned U/V
routine receives but does not read `khdif`; the captured nonzero `XKMHD`
carrier is active. Advection and assembled-RHS effects were kept separate.

## Initial wind lineage and scope

The archived C1 COUPLED candidate input is bound by the PR85 receipt to
`moment_prior_receipt.json` (SHA256
`64bc0cd3fffec7b72f324364b06449cc5719871380266fe0de0f9d6f8399720d`). That
prior records the unchanged real.exe input SHA256
`dce4a2633521639a4195babc461a45427f669dc808cb7b2d99d321b153b77afb`; the
moment-prior candidate supplied to the archived run is SHA256
`d10c2b987856586c9b8105a69eaeda03951f85f29be3ec4e7118d4f79b478c2d`.
The real input contract binds 13Z/14Z/15Z met_em files. The 13Z file is SHA256
`e635b4083c22258907338c06104c4ba8ef02a819e6b76a5c4f6cc567b067cd60` and
contains 21 pressure levels through 5000 Pa. The target initial faces are near
5280 Pa and 20.69 km, bracketed by the 10 and 5 kPa levels. Raw 5 kPa
staggered samples are −5.4373 m/s UU and −5.4991 m/s VV. The exact real.exe
vertical and horizontal transfer has not been replayed, so those raw samples
are not substitutes for the native face winds.

The PR99 projection changes U and V in place on their native staggered grids;
it performs no pressure-level interpolation or extrapolation. A same-time
met_em candidate was found, but its `real_exact_clone` U/V differs from the
current C1 COUPLED input and is not its parent. The source chain to real.exe
and the pressure-level bracket are known; the precise real.exe interpolation
that produced the native wind values remains unresolved. For adjacent mass
columns at the U/V targets, `P+PB` is about 5280 Pa and mass height is about
20.69 km; height is the average of adjacent `PH+PHB` interfaces divided by
`2g` (`g=9.81 m s-2`). PR100's native `(i,k,j)` values map to NetCDF
`[k-1,j-1,i-1]`; V `(135,17,39)` is `V[38,16,134]`.

## Validation and evidence

The source routine and generated capture driver compiled with pinned IFX 2026
strict O2, `-no-ftz`, `-real-size 32`, `-i4`, and `-convert big_endian`, in
fresh scratch directories. The common guarded replays for both arms returned
0, matched the POST bits, matched their expected output artifacts, printed the
PASS marker, and reported no preservation issues. The capture stream explicitly
uses `CONVERT='BIG_ENDIAN'`, as required by the C2 compile flags.

Noninterference checks found exact receipt-bound matches between current H
and the closed C2 H 640-second control for full KDM6 PRE/POST and PR67 payloads,
input XLAND, ten raw T0 history fields (`T,P,PB,PH,PHB,U,V,W,MU,MUB`) with
matching metadata, and the corresponding 16 derived T0 fields. The same
checks match current P20 to archived C1 P20; that is explicitly a
cross-executable state/observer comparison (instrumented C2-derived overlay
against archived C1 P20). No live C2 P640 payload was read.

An initial pair invocation omitted `--execute`; those two preflight-only
directories were left intact and not reused. The approved corrected runs used
fresh sibling roots. Build, launch, capture, object, comparison, and guarded
replay receipts are preserved in the evidence folder. The builder receipt's
staged parent modules are precompile seeds; `modules[].regenerated=true` and
`modules[].sha256` identify the postcompile outputs. Those historical seed
path/hash pairs should not be interpreted as claims about current bytes.

The focused capture/replay tests pass 12/12, `py_compile` passes, and
`git diff --check` passes. The Cloud-BAL graph covers diagnostic navigation,
not the external WRF dynamics call graph; graph freshness is not scientific
validation. No wind tuning, clipping, diffusion disabling, layer removal,
projection sweep, or longer forecast was performed. The native mass
constraint `A_k b_k = 0` is unchanged.
