## Cold-gate fixture

The manufactured test state uses T=250 K, P=90,000 Pa, 80% relative humidity over ice from the source saturation routine, and DELT=1 s. It does not alter native model settings. Its source-PSD parameters are cloud Q=2e-4 kg/kg and λ=100,000 m⁻¹; ice Q=2e-5 and λ=20,000 m⁻¹; rain Q=1e-4 and λ=5,000 m⁻¹. Source-derived number concentrations initialize those Q/λ pairs. Snow Q is 2e-5 kg/kg. Graupel uses QG=2e-5 kg/kg and BG=5e-8 m³/kg dry air, so QG/BG=400 kg/m³. The cold joint gate is exercised in the full module at dry density 0.25, 0.5, 1, and 2 kg/m³.

The same test also makes a matched-volume comparison: hydrometeor mass mixing ratios and public specific numbers scale as `1/rho_dry`, keeping volume quantities fixed. For every density and optimization level, the unit-converted candidate and the direct-volume control agree on the returned state and public number fields within the declared floating-point tolerance. This is a unit-conversion comparison for the manufactured state, not a claim that physical process tendencies are invariant as density changes.

The runner groups acceptance markers with each corresponding density result and requires cells `(1,1)`, `(2,1)`, and `(3,1)` to pass with a positive accepted fraction at every density. Across each executable, this yields 12 observed cold-gate acceptances: three cells at each of four densities. The test also asserts updates to temperature, vapor/cloud state, rain number, and graupel volume, and checks finite output plus public number back-conversion. It preserves the wide-fixture rejection separately; that fixture is not changed into a pass. The early wide-fixture rejection, invalid-ice-PSD trials, and a preliminary compile failure caused by the stock fixture overwriting the composed module are retained in `docs/evidence/pr70_unit_process_failures_20261008/receipt.json`.
