# PR98 — same-call cutoff contribution to C1 T1 water residual

This adds one independently observed term to the PR97 first-public-call water
budget. The new term is the direct change in the five Q stores at the frozen
KDM6 `qmin` / `qcrmin` cutoff assignments, read from their captured
before/after values. A store set to zero is counted as a threshold removal;
it is not labeled as rounding error or hidden inside the residual.

## Full-tile results

All values are kg. The fixed measure remains the input `DEN × DELZ × map area`
for the captured C1 call. `R_raw` includes the same-call actual surface outflow.
`C_cutoff` is the signed sum of actual Q-store changes at cutoff, independent
of `R_raw` and the liquid store error. `R_open` is the result after subtracting
both independently captured terms.

Cutoff events are actual removals from state, with 212,866 rows in OFF and
HYDRO, and 212,872 in COUPLED. They occur in 40,150 columns. The global signed
cutoff term accounts for part of `R_raw`; the residual stays open.

## Independent local evidence

The previously largest OFF unexplained column, `(i,j) = (184,147)`, has
`R_raw = −70.735488197`, `C_liquid_store = −9.611814260`, and direct cutoff
Q-store change `−60.567291093` kg. Its remaining value is `−0.556382843` kg.
The cutoff amount comes from source `Qbefore(5)` and `Qafter(5)` at each
existing assignment, weighted by the original fixed `DEN × DELZ × area`;
no endpoint residual is used to infer it. At this column the change is
`QI = −0.000000005336` kg and `QR = −60.567291088` kg; `QC`, `QS`, and `QG`
do not change.

The largest positive remaining column is `(208,79)` at `+37.628379908` kg.
It has effectively zero cutoff change, a rain Q-store residual of
`−0.017755862` kg, and an ice Q-store residual of `+7.372483194` kg. These
separate source-ledger terms have different signs and do not close that
column. At `(184,147)`, the rain and ice Q-store residuals are respectively
`+0.000000867` and `+0.012652639` kg. Their number-store residuals are
tracked separately and are not water terms.

This comparison shows why the cutoff contribution is useful while leaving
other processes and representation terms unassigned. The remaining signed,
L1 and maximum column values are reported separately for every arm, along
with the twenty largest original and remaining columns and the twenty largest
positive remaining columns.

## Replay identity and extraction limits

Each arm was replayed using the existing frozen C1 full-tile diagnostic
executable from a new empty directory with the pinned `tests/intel_toolchain.sh`
profile initialized, one OpenMP thread, and unlimited stack. The input was the
receipt-bound native T1 pre-state. Each replay returned all 17 fields and
`XLAND` byte-for-byte equal to that arm's receipt-bound native post-state. The
same executable SHA256 was used for all arms:

`94d3d35b1c204d00cc4df548521dc6ad4088b76e7d37db1a9a5d86da975bebd5`

The clean cutoff ledgers contain 212,866 OFF/HYDRO cutoff-event rows and
212,872 COUPLED cutoff-event rows. The raw files also contain 280 header or
non-event lines; those are not included in event counts. A prior append-contaminated OFF diagnostic
ledger contained exact duplicate records; it was excluded. No records were
deduplicated by value. The report records each replay's executable, pre/post,
liquid, cutoff, rain and ice hashes plus ledger row counts, binding those
terms to the same completed invocation and returned state.

## Accepted sediment-face/store follow-up

A second observer captured the actual accepted face packets in the frozen C1
component call and the donor and receiver Q values immediately before and
after each accepted update. For each face it evaluates the donor store change
plus the approved packet and the receiver store change minus that packet; a
ground face has no receiver. It then accumulates per-face errors with Neumaier
compensation and compares the per-column sums to the independently captured
rain and ice source-stage rows. This is an accepted-packet/store discrepancy,
including REAL32 representation and REAL64 accumulation effects; it is not
classified as pure REAL32 rounding or inferred from the remaining endpoint.

The resulting full-tile contribution is small compared with the remaining
budget and makes the signed residual slightly more negative after subtraction:

The selected `(208,79)` source with a positive ice-stage closure has a
`+7.354727 kg` accepted rain+ice face term, leaving `+30.273653 kg` after all
independent terms. The selected `(184,147)` face term is `+0.012654 kg`, leaving
`−0.569036 kg`. Thus these measured terms do not explain the larger residual.
The combined full-tile accepted-face term has absolute column sum about
12,047–12,054 kg; it is not equivalent to its much smaller signed sum.

The selected raw capture has 312 accepted face records: 78 ice, 117 graupel
and 117 rain. Of these, 81 have a positive approved packet (34 ice and 47
rain); 117 graupel records have zero approved packet. These are record and
packet counts, not a count of distinct physical transfers. The full-tile
summaries cover 3,078,114 rain and 2,533,440 ice accepted face records, with
1,090,147 nonzero approved packets. Event-level L1/max and column-level
L1/max are kept as different metrics.

The observer executable is separate from the frozen native executable and
was built from the pinned Intel profile in a fresh external scratch directory.
Each O2 full-tile replay completed and reproduced all 17 returned fields and
`XLAND` byte-for-byte. The face replay manifests pin executable, source,
inputs, outputs, source ledgers, history geometry and build receipt. O0 audit
build completed as well. A focused Intel O0/O2 manufactured test independently
checks the compensated sum `2^60 + 1 - 2^60 = 1`. The full-tile O0 execution
was stopped due its much higher diagnostic runtime and is not used as evidence.
The face manifests record the observed full-tile completion marker and exact
returned-state comparison; they do not claim a separately retained subprocess
exit code.

The reducer also reports the rain and ice sedimentation Q-stage residuals as
separate diagnostics. Their source definition is the paired operator's stored
Q change plus approved ground Q sink for one sedimentation substep; it is a
narrow operator closure check, not a proven additive whole-call term. The
remaining column is `R_raw - C_liquid - C_cutoff - C_face` and remains open.
Each arm report records the native history area source path, SHA256,
variable, and one-based active bounds.

The face follow-up adds a separately built read-only diagnostic observer, its
Python reducer and tests. No KDM6 physics, precision, tolerance or state was
changed. Full-tile residual accounting does not establish runtime or
scientific closure for other KDM6 terms, the native host hybrid water budget,
energy, mass-wind balance, or observations. The bounded arm reports and their
capture identities, replay manifests and independent review are in
[the evidence directory](evidence/pr98_t1_residual_20261022/).
