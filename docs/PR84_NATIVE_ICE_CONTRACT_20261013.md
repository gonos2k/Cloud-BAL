# PR84 native KDM6 ice representation contract

## Finding

The PR83 prior's joint requirement `lambda_i >= 9,080 m-1` and internal `NI <= 1,000,000 m-3` is a conservative prior-domain rule, not a persistent invariant of the retained KDM6 consumer. In the retained source, KDM6 clips incoming internal ice number to `1e6`, then reconstructs the PSD slope from ice mass and number. If that slope is below `lambda_i_min`, it sets the slope to `9,080` and recomputes ice number from mass. For QI above about `2.098 g m-3` (using the PR83 source constants and dry-carrier density), that recomputed number exceeds the entry cap. The end-of-call slope reconstruction repeats this adjustment and there is no later `1e6` clamp before the internal number is converted back to the public NI field.

The 2,305-cell PR83 transaction rejection therefore says the selected prior cannot simultaneously satisfy its own PSD interval and entry-cap intersection. It does not show that KDM6 rejects these ice masses. PR83 remains a valid record of that declared prior and its rejection; this investigation does not rewrite or supersede its receipt.

At the `kdm6` interface, public `QI` remains mass mixing ratio and public `NI` is specific number per dry-air mass. The PR80 wrapper converts NI to internal number concentration with the source dry density before passing it as `nci(:,:,2)` to `kdm62D`, then converts the resulting internal concentration back to specific NI. Within `kdm62D`:

- Entry cleanup clamps `nci(i,k,2)` to `[0, 1e6]` at line 1564.
- Before the first QI-based NI reconstruction, KDM6 copies QI/NI into the slope inputs, calls `slope_kdm6`, computes number fall rates, and runs ice sedimentation (lines 1815-2029). `den_tmp` is set to the dry carrier density at line 1615. `slope_kdm6` clamps the derived slope to the configured interval but does not rewrite NI. Thus ice number fall rates and the first sedimentation update consume the entry-clipped NI.
- For `QI >= 1e-14`, lines 2307-18 derive `lambda_i` from `pidni * NI / (dry_density * QI)`. If lambda is at or below `lambda_i_min`, KDM6 sets it to `lambda_i_min` and recalculates NI as `dry_density * QI * lambda_i**3 / pidni`. This is a mass-preserving PSD projection that can exceed `1e6`.
- The same lower/upper endpoint reconstruction repeats at lines 2512-23 after ice nucleation/freezing updates, and again at lines 4075-83 near the end of the call. None reapplies the entry `1e6` clamp.
- Later ice number sources include cloud-to-ice nucleation and freezing (lines 2342, 2372); deposition nucleation adds `ninud` in the cold-process update. Number sinks include sedimentation (2006-29), conversion/autoconversion `nsaut`, ice collected by rain/snow/graupel (`nraci`, `nsaci`, `ngaci`), and ice collection of rain (`niacr` is a rain-number sink). Ice may also melt into cloud (2145-50) or be converted to snow by the end-of-call size branch (3847-51), both zeroing the ice number with the ice mass.
- The cold-process mass and number update at lines 3633-39 removes QI for `psaut`, `praci`, `psaci`, `pgaci`, deposition/nucleation and other coupled terms; NI is updated with the corresponding source/sink rates. The late slope reconstruction at lines 4075-83 again clips lambda to the source interval and recomputes NI from QI at an endpoint. It does not apply the `1e6` entry clamp again.
- Output conversion at lines 1078-83 maps internal ice concentration back to public NI. No output-side `1e6` clamp was found.

The upstream KIM-meso source has the same entry clamp. Immediately above it, the source comment says it is an upper bound intended “to prevent very large ice number due to homogeneous freezing of droplets.” This is a scheme guard motivated by a process pathology. It is not documented as an observed or universal physical maximum. `lambda_i_min` is used as a slope endpoint in the ice PSD reconstruction and `rslope` calculation; the source does not describe it as a separate all-state mass ceiling. Together they are not a stable state contract because the source itself reprojects NI to the lambda endpoint after the cap.

## Existing ice-to-snow rules

KDM6 has two materially different mechanisms that can move ice mass to snow.

The cold-process `psaut` rate (actual source lines 3259-69) is active only inside the `supcol > 0` branch and for positive QI and NI. It calculates a temperature- and `supcol`-dependent `qi0` with a branch at `T=255.66 K`, sets `alpha1 = 0.005 * exp(0.025 * supcol)`, and uses the assumed spherical ice mass `Miaut = pi * 500 * (125e-6)**3 / 6`. The mass rate is `min(max(0, alpha1*(QI-qi0)), QI/dt)`. Its NI sink is `min(psaut*dry_density/Miaut, NI/dt)`. The coupled cold-process limiter can scale these rates along with other processes. In the final update, QI loses `psaut*dt`, QSNOW gains that same mass, and NI loses `nsaut*dt`; there is no prognostic snow-number field to receive number. The final temperature/enthalpy expression contains phase-change and liquid/solid transfer terms but not `psaut`: ice-to-snow is solid-to-solid, so this branch does not alter temperature.

A separate end-of-call branch transfers all remaining QI to QSNOW when `QI >= qmin`, `T < 273.15 K`, and diagnosed ice mean diameter `avedia >= 200e-6 m` (actual source lines 3842-51). It sets QI and NI to zero and adds QI to snow mass. This is also solid-to-solid, with no temperature change and no snow number transfer. The ice mean diameter is `rslope_i * (Gamma(4)/Gamma(1))**(1/3)`. With `mu_i=0`, `d_i=3`, and `lambda_i >= 9,080 m-1`, the greatest diameter permitted by the slope clamp is `6**(1/3)/9080 = 181.7 µm`. Thus the `200 µm` branch is algebraically unreachable under the configured slope bound. This is a source-based inference, not a runtime observation.

Other collection pathways are not interchangeable with either rule: `psaci` removes ice mass and number into snow; `pgaci` removes them into graupel; rain accretion `praci` partitions collected ice mass between snow and graupel using `delta3`, while its number sink is bounded by available ice number. These are collection rates requiring existing collector PSDs and fall-speed/efficiency state. They do not define an unconditional initialization redistribution. The separate `psaut` threshold is also a tendency formula, not a rule to remove all mass above `qi0`; its result depends on dt, temperature, supersaturation, PSD state, and the coupled rate limiter. The source comparison uses `qci` with the implemented `qi0` expression, which includes gas density; this investigation does not independently establish dimensional or observational validation for that parameterization.

## V2 choices supported by this evidence

1. **Use a public-storage-compatible source bound while retaining the low-IWC guard.** The authorized PR84 patch computes the exact public PSD minimum `Nmin = QI * lambda_i_min**dmi / pidni` in REAL64, upward-rounds `dry_density * Nmin` to the minimum REAL32 internal floor, then finds the smallest REAL32 public NI that is both at least `Nmin` in REAL64 and whose source REAL32 multiplication by dry density reaches that internal floor. The entry ceiling is `max(1e6, REAL32(NI_min * dry_density))`. Bounded eight-step `NEAREST` searches fail closed if minimality or range cannot be established. This retains the original low-IWC homogeneous-freezing guard, and prevents a minimally representable source-PSD-valid public number from being clipped at entry. It changes neither QI nor temperature nor hydrometeor class. This is a storage-compatibility guard, not an observed number estimate or scientific prior.
2. **Normalize before any number-consuming process.** A source-order fix could perform the existing lambda endpoint projection before ice sedimentation, so the rate sees the source-supported number. It preserves the current cap code but needs a deliberate source change and order/conservation review because it moves a reconstruction ahead of existing sedimentation.
3. **Retain the strict PR83 prior as a research admission gate.** Continue to reject any transaction with cells outside the joint lambda/cap set. This is internally coherent as a chosen prior, but its result must be described as prior infeasibility, not native KDM6 state rejection.
4. **Consider mass transfer only under a separately justified rule.** The existing 200 µm conversion cannot trigger within the configured lambda interval. `psaut` is a process rate that should not be turned into a static excess-cap transfer without a separately justified time/state/energy policy. A new transfer rule needs physical evidence and conservation/readback review; do not move just the excess above `2.098 g m-3` to snow.
