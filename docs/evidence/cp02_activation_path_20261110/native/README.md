# Three-point weak-rain native capture

The paired CONTROL (PR154 physics) and MP10 diagnostic forecasts ran for three hours with unchanged physical source parents. Both were built in fresh scratch directories using the pinned Intel profile. Each arm's four histories at 0, 1, 2 and 3 hours are byte-identical to its six-hour parent histories. The insertion-only source receipts, build and probe receipts, histories, exact comparisons and lossless packing are bound by `proof/comparability.json`.

Three adjacent observation points (12000, 12001 and 12002) map to eight unique native columns: j=86–87 and i=155–158, one-based. The complete 39-level process and selected-wrapper records cover outer steps 361–540; surface records cover steps 1–540. The five columns shared with PR158 match every common process record's key and native IEEE bits and both wrapper stages across all 180 detailed steps in each arm. The remaining three columns are new. Outer step and actual inner loop identify records; conditional records that do not exist remain missing, not zero.

Phases 35 and 36 capture, immediately before and after activation, `sw`, `NC`, `NN`, `q`, `qc`, `t`, `den`, `xl`, `cpm`, `dtcld`, `pi` and `denr`. The pre/post sample packets in `proof` preserve exact REAL32 words and their raw-stream hashes. Phases 30, 32, 33 and 34 have a different 18-field layout. `schema/contract_supplement.json` binds the reviewed schema and corrects its inherited descriptive range and state-field ambiguity. The untouched PR158 schema has stale prose: its actual detailed windows were 1–180, 361–540 and 901–1080.

The packed arrays use complete keyed REAL32 bits (and REAL64 sedimentation-face bits) in lossless ZIP-LZMA NPZ files; wrapper streams are lossless gzip. The recount compares every retained key and payload bit to the raw streams. CONTROL retains 1,379,104 process, surface and frame-constant records; MP10 retains 2,625,705; each also retains 960 byte-identical wrapper streams. Each packed arm contains 1,380 artifacts. The redundant, audited pre-packing files are under `scratch/b1_activation_path_20261110/runtime/unpacked_compact`; their relocation receipts retain their hashes. Raw model and probe streams remain in `scratch/b1_activation_path_20261110/runtime/forecasts` and `runtime/probe`.

The all-step surface audit replayed 4,315 CONTROL and 8,524 MP10 executed rain increments in source-order REAL32, and verified 12,960 MP10 solid-counter carries. Snow and graupel counters overlap total precipitation and must not be added as independent totals. A missing positive-fallsum branch does not measure a zero fallsum. These tests establish reproducibility of the selected diagnostic capture, not a domain water or energy budget, other cases, or causal attribution.
