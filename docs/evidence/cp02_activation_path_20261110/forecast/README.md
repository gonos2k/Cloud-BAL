`MP10_NN_END` changes one statement in the frozen MP10 source: the aerosol-number lower bound of 1e8 m⁻³ is applied at the end of the final inner microphysics loop, rather than at the end of every inner loop. The existing outer-entry lower bound and the 2e10 m⁻³ upper bound in every inner loop are unchanged, as are the 10-second internal step, all other PSD limits, coefficients and initial fields. `source/source_receipt.json` binds the exact parent and generated source. This is one numerical-policy sensitivity, not coefficient tuning or an adopted forecast change.

The frozen `policy.json` preserves all 11 B1-protection and 15 ORIGINAL-recovery conditions, evaluated separately as exact CSI comparisons. Six lead hours, six thresholds and all old comparison cases remain in scope. The scorer and its independent preforecast review were frozen before candidate data inspection; `launch_plan.json` binds those artifacts and the production build, probe and staged inputs.

A pinned-Intel source-extracted kernel exercised below-floor and above-cap values for three 20-second policies. CONTROL uses one internal loop and one inner floor, MP10 uses two loops and two inner floors, and the candidate uses two loops and one final inner floor; the upper cap applies on every loop. On the deliberately below-floor trial, candidate NN is 8e7 m⁻³ after loop 1 and 1e8 m⁻³ after loop 2. This reduced kernel is not a full KDM6 replay.

A separate, trace-only full-scheme 20-second WRF probe observed two 10-second loops at one selected native cell. Its entry clamp restored NN from 41,902,956 to 100,000,000 m⁻³; the observed inner-loop NN values stayed at the bound, so the trace confirms actual loop and branch timing but does not exercise a below-floor or above-cap inner state. The selected-cell trace and the synthetic bound tests are distinct evidence.
