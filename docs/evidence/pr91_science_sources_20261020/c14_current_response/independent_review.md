Review scope: receipt identity, cold-activity area join and interpretation, native history grid/time alignment, and hybrid dry-mass inventory construction. This is an evidence review; it does not validate physical correctness or approve the candidate.

## Findings

- **No blocker found.** Both archived run receipts are `COMPLETE`, return code 0, with 12 completed steps and 240-second receipt windows. Both identify executed executable SHA-256 `5581e1be3febd54409304ccf7cbe305830a75968858075bf0d95f68b48d812be`; the cold summary also binds the candidate build, compiled KDM source, stage trace, history and receipt hashes per arm.
- The cross-arm `wrfinput_d01` hashes differ by design. Each C14 arm uses its own frozen analysis-arm input; that difference alone does not invalidate the comparison. I independently checked the C14 OFF and COUPLED input hashes against the same-arm C7 180-second receipts: both are unchanged. Boundary input hashes match across arms. The current note correctly describes a matched OFF/COUPLED analysis response under C14 and limits claims about observational skill, closed budgets, and pure-model causal effects.
- `pr90_cold_column_summary.area_weighted_totals` is the appropriate join. It validates the receipt/build/executable and compiled source identities; binds the raw column summary, history and stage records to that run; checks single-process/single-tile execution; checks PBL and consumer bounds; requires all expected interior columns at each completed timestep; verifies the same-run history window; and confirms map factors are static through the history window. The C14 rows cover 64,960 of 64,960 expected interior columns for every completed step on the 234×282 mass grid.
- The cold activity arithmetic is a diagnostic request measure. The source forms `signed=(sum(q_request)+qv_request)*scale` and `half_l1=0.5*(sum(abs(q_request))+abs(qv_request))*abs(scale)`, with `scale=1` for proposed, `alpha` for applied, and `1-alpha` for deferred. It weights by same-call `dry_carrier_density*DELZ` (the source assigns `dry_carrier_density=DEN`) and then by `DX*DY/(MAPFAC_MX*MAPFAC_MY)`. This produces kg-valued mixing-ratio request activity per model timestep, summed over KDM calls and columns. Applied/deferred are the acceptance-factor partition of proposed request activity, not separately measured stored-endpoint deltas or realized reaction throughput, precipitation, or an inventory budget. The revised note states these limits. Its 1–12 totals reproduce the table values.
- For each arm, the receipt-bound history reaches elapsed 0/60/120/180/240 seconds. Direct inspection found the pressure, geopotential, and W arrays have shapes `(39,282,234)`, `(40,282,234)`, and `(40,282,234)` respectively at all five times; `P+PB` is at mass levels, `PH+PHB` at interfaces, and W at vertical faces. The OFF/COUPLED static-grid validator passed exact coordinate, map-factor, staggered-coordinate, and vertical-coordinate comparisons. Thus the report preserves the native staggerings rather than comparing a mass-point proxy for W.
- The reported water inventories use the existing hybrid dry-mass calculation at each arm and time: `dp=-(C1H*(MU+MUB)+C2H)*DNW`; cell mass is `DX*DY/(MAPFAC_MX*MAPFAC_MY)*dp/g`, and each hydrometeor mixing ratio is weighted by that mass. The summary includes all-native and interior totals at the same five elapsed times. This is a time-varying native-hybrid inventory diagnostic, not a full water budget.

## Limits

The contrast remains descriptive because OFF and COUPLED are separately frozen analysis-arm inputs, even though same-arm C7 input identities are preserved. No observation fit, full process closure, causal treatment effect, or physical approval follows from these diagnostics. Inventory totals do not include precipitation/export terms, and cold ledger activity is not reaction throughput.
