Review findings
- No concrete blocker found in the proposed U/V projection, receipt/runner
  binding, native response calculation, or reported units.
- The control mask has 196 fixed QC-buffer mass columns. A U or V face is free
  only when both adjacent columns are in that mask. The incidence matrix gives
  each free face equal and opposite contributions to its two cells, so each
  column sums to zero. Exterior and buffer-boundary increments remain fixed.
- Per-level coefficients use the frozen REAL32 carrier construction and
  calc_ww_cp geometry ordering, then promote to REAL64 for the linear solve.
  The candidate is formed by projecting only COUPLED minus HYDRO U/V and adding
  the projected increment back to HYDRO; therefore the constraint preserves
  the HYDRO reference divergence rather than imposing zero total flux. The
  generator checks that non-U/V variables and metadata match the COUPLED
  parent, writes only U/V, and binds candidate, parent, HYDRO reference,
  boundary, generator, and QC composition hashes in its proposal receipt.
- The runner treats this as a COUPLED-only proposal with a 20-second limit.
  It verifies every receipt artifact, matches parent and boundary to the
  frozen COUPLED template, stages the bound candidate, and checks the same
  launch binding at finalization. The response analyzer requires a clean
  complete one-step receipt and verifies its source/build/input/boundary,
  all 57 snapshots, and the exact staged state comparison before analysis.
- The mass-rate conversion is dimensionally kg/s: fixed-geometry hybrid
  inventory difference is area/g times sum(-C1H*DNW) times the observed MU
  endpoint difference per captured RK second. It promotes REAL32 endpoints
  before subtraction, checks the captured hybrid profile and bounds, and uses
  native map area plus receipt-pinned gravity. It is labeled as a local
  footprint response, not a closed face-flux or global mass budget.
- The response report keeps RU/RV in distinct native source-coordinate units;
  it does not combine them as acceleration or energy. The public docs state
  the one-step scope, lack of observation-skill validation, and FAIL_OPEN
  decision. The candidate result is not presented as closing the long-run
  grouped failure.
