# Paired producer and native initialization

A fresh private export of the reviewed Cloud-BAL source, with four recorded policy source files, was compiled using the pinned Intel O2 profile and the external KLAPS workspace. `build_receipt.json` identifies the source pins, build artifacts and resulting executable. The builder hashes its declared compile and link inputs, checks the final link and runtime closure, and records exact commands. The separate compiler-process tracing closure was not assessed because the pinned tracer was unavailable.

Both producer modes used detached copies of the same retained HYDRO preparation tree. Every one of its 114 regular files, including payload, cdl, time and log, was hashed before and after copying and launch. Both source and staged files remained unchanged. These new prelaunch seals do not establish the earlier historical contents of the 31 files that were not presealed then. Both modes used the same executable, namelist and source data; the environment selects HYDRO or HYDRO_BACKGROUND_PRECIP. The normal HYDRO WPS and shadow bytes exactly match the retained historical outputs. Both producer receipts bind their executable, full input inventory and output hashes.

`policy_scoped_check.json` is the dedicated checker result, bound to the candidate shadow SHA in the producer receipt and checker SHA in the build receipt. It checks the policy-specific precipitation and phase values, validity, quality and source arrays, zero radar flux/support and disabled effective precipitation transport. Its PASS applies only to that scoped diagnostic and does not establish full-model conservation, continuity, physical balance, forecast skill or operational authority. The shadow remains DIAGNOSTIC_PROPOSAL_ONLY. The general shadow validator intentionally rejects this research-policy schema.

Both WPS products went through privately staged historical metgrid and real executables, with per-file pre/post input and source checks. Native baseline met_em and wrfinput exactly match the retained HYDRO hashes; policy wrfinput was produced through metgrid and real, not by changing a WRF tracer field. The native receipts and logs record completion and output hashes. Native 208-field, pressure/geopotential and first-hour score assessment are independent work; no WRF forecast was launched here.

Full scratch artifacts are in `/var/tmp/firsthour-runtime-plan-20261030/producer_pair/`. The prior first-hour forecasts and their evidence are retained separately.
