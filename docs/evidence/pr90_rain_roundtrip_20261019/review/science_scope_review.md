Thus the supported conclusion is “no validated mapping is available in the reviewed evidence; the original support remains unmapped,” not that a map is mathematically impossible. The two PR90 documents preserve that distinction and do not manufacture a map. A direct link from the main PR90 paragraph to the gap audit would make the evidence trail easier to follow.
