# Independent source and interpretation review

The recorded KDM6 driver source at `docs/evidence/cp02_initial_boundaries_20261105/native/sourcepins/module_microphysics_driver.f90` selects `KDM6SCHEME` at line 2269 and calls KDM6 with `RAIN=rainnc` and `RAINNCV=rainncv` at line 2305. Its SHA-256 `149e11e0dc678600f189bd5fb66c190b6253b0663b253d211d929d8b17b0cddc` matches the frozen grouped-build `source/lineage/phys_module_microphysics_driver.f90`. The first-ice native build recipe checks that frozen input manifest and replaces the KDM6 object in the frozen archive; matching source lineage alone does not prove the archive's driver was compiled from that source.

In the archived candidate KDM6 source, `rainncv` is reset at each call (line 787), and the same positive bottom fall-sum depth is added to the per-call `rainncv` and accumulating `rain` (lines 1293–1300). These outputs concern RAINNC, while RAINC is a distinct counter outside that KDM6 return. Prior captured native surface returns have been cross-bound to original RAINNC history (see `tools/cp02_rain_number_process.py:556` and the associated evidence), supporting the linkage without asserting that this new diagnostic records an individual causal process.

The forecast verification uses successive hourly differences of RAINC + RAINNC at the frozen bilinear points. It measures the preceding hour, not a six-hour total, and includes total precipitation rather than snow alone. Separate stored counter differences are descriptive contributions to that total, not a causal decomposition: rain generation, transport, evaporation, numerical evolution and feedbacks cannot be distinguished by hourly surface counters. Bucket counters, resets, RAINSH, missing values and interpolation order must be checked on the actual histories; the independent analysis should not infer those checks from source names or graph freshness.

The fixed +6 h PR153 false-alarm cohort is selected on observation <5 mm, B1 <5 mm and PR153 >=5 mm. Its 65 persistent and 37 resolved points, plus 26 newly entering this same B1-conditioned cohort, are not all-point PR153-to-candidate transitions: points where B1 >=5 mm are excluded from the fixed cohort. The full false-alarm population must instead be restricted only by observation <5 mm, giving 48 gained and 50 lost for PR153-to-candidate and 91 gained and 32 lost for B1-to-candidate. These are descriptive paired grid-point comparisons in one case; they do not establish independent forecast skill or a physical mechanism.
