The 68 initial OpenMP rows are described as thread observations, not 68 successful calls; the 299,933 moment-only pairs, 740 mass-only pairs, and 23,494 negative/invalid pairs are expressly state-class counts, not tests, case counts, or meteorological error rates (`PR77_INTEGRATION_PROGRESS` lines 68–77). The two native arms are both rejected. PR76's “68 Python test methods” are solver regressions, not physical cases (`PR76_PHYSICAL_CLOSURE_CHECKLIST` line 14). Preserve those labels in any summary.

The older PR62–76 reports contain many PASS_SCOPED unit, solver, process, observer, and serialization findings. Their own scope language and physical closure matrices keep native/science conditions open. In particular, PR73's endpoint path is not an accepted normal SHADOW candidate; PR74–76 native attempts were rejected; PR77 supersedes the earlier PR76 observer experiment but confirms rejection. Historical “run_cloud_bal_pipeline not run” statements in PR74/PR76 remain accurate for those snapshots and are superseded, rather than contradicted, by PR77's new endpoint assessment run. Do not aggregate those scoped passes into a physical acceptance percentage.
