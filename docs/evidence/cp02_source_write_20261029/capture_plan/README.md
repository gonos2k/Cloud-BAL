# Source writer capture

The frozen helper captures the pinned executable and selection under the Intel profile in a fresh scratch copy, stopping after the first public KDM6 outer call after a two-phase all-rank settlement. OFF, HYDRO and LIQUID each have eight settled rank records; rank 1 owns P05_00 and rank 3 owns P01_00. The context, run receipt and hashes bind each capture. `runtime/prepare.json` records the scratch inputs. Collector receipts include original and copied input hashes, executable, runtime mappings and intentional MPI launcher exit.

For each target the validated inner call provides the QR address. At 0x25ef187 the helper checks that frame+0x1f0 equals the inner QR base and that the target offset is aligned and in bounds. It arms a hardware access watch at frame+0x728 plus that offset. It checks that this is the actual frame+0xc0 indexed source address at 0x25f4492. The watch covers reads and writes, including same-value stores, from arming through the inner return, but does not cover allocation and initialization before arming. GDB confirms `Hardware access (read/write) watchpoint`; its `info breakpoints` reports `acc watchpoint`, and the helper verifies `gdb.BP_ACCESS_WATCHPOINT`. QR, q, and t retain write watches; qci is observed at entry and return without a continuous watch.

Explicitly guarded instruction hooks capture the two statically identified source stores at 0x25ef2a8 and 0x25ef98f and their next instructions. The upstream 0x25ef2cf, 0x25ef2f6, 0x25ef2fb, 0x25ef29d and 0x25ef97a probes capture frame bytes, scalar registers and raw operands. Producer and preclamp hooks retain the prior capture. All source, producer, preclamp, QR/q/t watch events, and inner and outer boundaries share a per-rank monotonic event ordinal; inner events identify the inner call. Access-watch and software-breakpoint callbacks at the same next PC may be delivered in debugger order, so their ordinal is callback order, not instruction order. These records concern only the selected cells and first call; they do not establish completed forecasts or scientific validation.

The preliminary OFF write-watch attempt settled but lacked the final access watch and upstream probes; its logs and frozen helper are preserved in `captures/attempt_initial_OFF`, with raw records remaining in the scratch tree named by its receipt. Two subsequent OFF access-watch attempts failed setup because the helper rejected GDB's access-watch label; their error records and logs are preserved in `captures/attempt_access_OFF_failed` and `captures/attempt_access_label_OFF_failed`. Neither failed attempt is used as final evidence.
