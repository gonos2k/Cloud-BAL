The former carries the per-level comment “Cloud Ice Content - LAPS Smith Feddes”; the latter says “Snow Content”. These establish the product's declared categories and analysis lineage, not an independent observation of a KDM6 particle-size distribution or proof that its cloud-ice category excludes every frozen aggregate. We found no independently supported replacement partition or nonzero ice-number/PSD in the inspected B1 initialization chain. Retain strict rejection of an infeasible joint state until such authority is supplied; neither the cap nor these labels alone selects a redistribution.
