## Physical inventory and observation limits

The full stage trace records same-state dry density (`DEN`), but does not record layer thickness (`DELZ`) at PRE_RK and POST_KDM6 endpoints. `DELZ` exists in the first-call KDM6 PRE/POST captures only. Static cell area is available from the initial WRF history record's `DX`, `DY`, `MAPFAC_MX`, and `MAPFAC_MY`. Without endpoint DELZ for every step, the requested `rhoDry * DELZ * area` Q/N/B inventory deltas cannot be computed for the two full steps without assuming fixed layer thickness. No two-step kg, particle, or column inventory residual is reported.

The only WRF history record is the initialization time, 2026-08-16 13:00:00. It includes initial pressure (`P`, `PB`), geopotential (`PH`, `PHB`), wind (`U`, `V`, `W`), and map fields; there are no post-step pressure, geopotential, or wind fields and no observational or echo data. Therefore no V42 observation/echo response proxy is supported by the saved outputs.

This analysis uses the V42 receipts and source manifest only. It does not reuse the older v17 PR86 residual. Science validation remains open.
