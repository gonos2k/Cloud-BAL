## Compact observer and noninterference proposal

Use a gated, read-only observation at the RK scalar consumer boundary: one timestep, every active RK stage, one declared domain/tile or a small immutable cell set. Start with scalar sums/min/max and counts for entry state, dry carrier, native flux components, and tendency-source components; record per-stage identity, options, bounds, weights, and checksums. To make transport interpretable, add actual scheme face fluxes only for the selected support (inside the chosen advection routine after its face reconstruction/limiting), with a net-divergence check. Do not recompute tendencies or write model arrays. Keep I/O buffered or emit one compact record per stage.

Before interpreting budgets, compare baseline and observer-enabled runs from the same frozen executable/input. Require bitwise identity for model prognostic outputs and relevant history/trajectory products through the observed step, compare control-flow/cell counters, and check observer-disabled behavior. Separately reconcile stage updates and timestep totals in consistent dry-air mass units. A compact observer is plausible but its read-only nature and run noninterference are unvalidated until this A/B check; source inspection/build success is insufficient.
