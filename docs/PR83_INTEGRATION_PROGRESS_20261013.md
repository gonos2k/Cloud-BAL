The new initializer constructs cloud, ice and rain number moments and graupel volume from a declared PSD prior on a copy of the retained 13 UTC real output, before WRF starts. A successful copy must preserve the source mass, thermodynamic fields, coordinates and outer ring, and pass independent moment readback before launch. It is a one-time cold-start input transaction, not a recurring KDM6 repair or a `start_em.F` source hook. Snow remains mass-only and ambient CCN remains separate.

The actual retained candidate **cannot pass this prior**. The source ice PSD requires `lambda_i >= 9080 m^-1`, while the native internal ice-number cap is `10^6 m^-3`. Their combination allows at most `0.002098274638742861 kg m^-3` of ice. The actual dry density is the REAL32 reciprocal of REAL32 `AL+ALB`, following the retained consumer carrier. There are **2,305 infeasible active interior cells** (2,326 over the full array). At the first active failure, WRF `[i,j,k]=[195,73,17]`, the smallest allowable internal number is `1,095,670.895959 m^-3`. An initializer-independent, 80-digit calculation confirms the bound and cell counts.

The global transaction rejected before writing a candidate or launching native. Feasible local proposals were not committed; all committed counts are zero and the source hash is unchanged. A positive manufactured copy exercises successful moment construction, preservation, storage and readback. It is not an accepted real candidate.
